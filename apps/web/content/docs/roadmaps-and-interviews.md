Both features use the same text box as [learning paths](/docs/learning-paths): a topic, a goal, or a whole job description.

## Plan my roadmap

**Plan my roadmap** orders DevPilot's **ready-made labs** into a plan that fits your goal. It only chooses from the lab catalogue; it does not invent labs. Use it when you want a plan made of real, checked labs. Use **Build my learning path** when you want explanations, videos and quick checks around each step.

The roadmap shows the steps, why each is there, and the order. Saved roadmaps appear with your other data and are private to you.

## Interview questions

**Interview questions** writes practice questions from the text you paste, usually a job description.

You get **4 to 10 questions**. Each has:

- the **kind** of question,
- a **hint**, and
- **a good answer covers…**, a short list of points to hit.

The hint and the points stay **hidden until you open a question**, so you can try first. Tick **I practised this one** to track what you have done.

:::note Practice only
Interview answers are **not graded**. They never affect a score or a credential. The questions are saved with your own data; another person never sees them.
:::

## Tips

- Paste the real job description, not a summary. The questions follow its wording.
- Do the interview questions and the learning path together: the path teaches the skills, and the questions test whether you can explain them.
- Voice practice and a mock-interview timer are possible later. They are not in this preview.
