This walks through the first session. It takes about an hour, most of it the lab.

## 1. Sign in

Start DevPilot, accept the privacy notice, and sign up or sign in. You land on the **Dashboard**: where you left off, this week's checks, your streak and your paths.

![The DevPilot dashboard](/app/dashboard.webp)

## 2. Say what you want to learn

Open **Paths** in the left rail. There is one text box:

- A **short line** is treated as a topic or goal: *understand how Git branches work*.
- A **long or multi-line text** is treated as a job description. Paste a whole posting if you have one.

Pick **Beginner** or **Intermediate**, pick the language for the explanations (nine are available), and press **Build my learning path**.

![The Paths page](/app/paths.webp)

In a few seconds you get a path of 3 to 8 steps. Open the first one.

## 3. Work through a step

Every step has four tabs:

1. **Learn:** a short explanation and the key points.
2. **Watch:** one or two videos, played inside the app. You need to watch **80 percent**, and it is measured.
3. **Quick check:** three questions, and you need two right.
4. **Practice:** a lab. Open one from the catalogue, or create one (see [Create a lab with AI](/docs/create-a-lab-with-ai)).

When all three checkpoints are done, the step is complete and the next one unlocks. Details: [Learning paths](/docs/learning-paths).

## 4. Do your first lab

Open **Catalog** and choose **Hello, Python** (beginner, about 45 minutes). Press **Start this lab**.

The first time, DevPilot shows a plan: *Install the DevPilot Guide in Visual Studio Code?* It installs a small extension into your VS Code. Nothing is installed until you approve.

Then VS Code opens on your lab folder (`~/DevPilotLabs/python-hello-001`), with the **DevPilot Guide** in the right-hand side panel. Follow the steps, write the function, and press **Run check**. The tests run on your computer, and each step turns green when its check passes.

![A lab in the app](/app/lab.webp)

Stuck? Ask the tutor in the app. It gives hints, not the answer. See [Tutor and coach](/docs/tutor-and-coach).

## 5. Claim your credential

When every check in a lab passes, open **Credentials** and press **Claim credential**. It says plainly how the work was checked: *Completed, checked on your computer*. You can share it with a link. Details: [Credentials](/docs/credentials).

![The Credentials page](/app/credentials.webp)
