A **learning path** is an ordered list of 3 to 8 steps that takes you from where you are to a goal. You get one by typing a goal on the **Paths** page. It is planned by AI (see [How AI is used](/docs/how-ai-is-used)) and saved to your account, so you can leave and come back.

## Ask for a path

The Paths page has one text box with three buttons.

- **A topic or goal** is a short single line, for example *understand how Git branches work* or *become a junior Python backend developer*.
- **A job description** is a long or multi-line text. Paste the whole posting. A small note under the box tells you which of the two DevPilot decided it is.
- **Level:** Beginner or Intermediate.
- **Language:** the language of the explanations. Nine are available: English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali and Gujarati. Commands, code and file names stay in English.

The three buttons are:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Build my learning path** | Plans the steps and teaches each one (this page). |
| **Plan my roadmap** | Orders DevPilot's ready-made labs into a plan. See [Roadmaps and interview practice](/docs/roadmaps-and-interviews). |
| **Interview questions** | Writes practice questions from the text. Same page as above. |

:::note What is sent
The text you type is sent to the DevPilot service, with passwords and keys removed first. The page says so under the buttons. See [Security and privacy](/docs/security-and-privacy).
:::

## What you get

First an **outline**: a title, a short summary, and 3 to 8 steps. Each step has a title, a one-line objective and up to four skills it covers. Then, when you open a step, DevPilot builds its content.

Your paths are listed on the Paths page under **Your learning paths**, with a progress bar and a **Continue** button. The trash icon deletes one.

## A step in four tabs

Opening a step shows four tabs.

### Learn

A short explanation and the key points to remember.

### Watch

One or two videos, played **inside the app**. Watching is measured: you need to reach **80 percent**. See [Watching videos](/docs/watching-videos).

### Quick check

Three questions about the step. You need **two correct**. You can retry.

### Practice

A lab for the step. DevPilot offers one of two things:

- a **catalogue lab** that fits the topic (see [Labs](/docs/labs)), or
- a **topic for a new lab**, which you can have written for you. See [Create a lab with AI](/docs/create-a-lab-with-ai).

## Finishing a step

A step is complete when all three checkpoints are done:

1. the video is watched to 80 percent,
2. the quick check is passed (2 of 3), and
3. the lab is completed (every required check passes).

Then the next step **unlocks**. Steps open one at a time, so you cannot skip ahead. The path screen shows each step as done, open or locked, and a journey line with a pulsing marker on your current step.

## Where the videos come from

Videos are chosen **only from YouTube search results** (the YouTube Data API). The AI picks among the results; it does not invent links. Results are cached for a week, so the same topic loads faster the second time.

## Good to know

- Planning takes a few seconds for the outline and a little longer for each step.
- If the AI service is down, DevPilot says so in plain words and the rest of the app, including labs, keeps working.
- There are per-person daily limits on AI use, to keep the service affordable for everyone.
- Paths are private to your account. Another person on the same computer does not see them.
