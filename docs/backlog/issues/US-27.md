## User story
As a student, when AI fails I get a clear message and everything else keeps working.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-AI-010`: The provider layer shall classify failures as authentication, quota/rate limit, network, timeout or outage; shall retry only within a bounded policy; and shall not repeatedly retry an invalid credential.
- [ ] `FR-AI-011`: When AI is unavailable, the guide, editor, terminal, practice checks and verified grading shall remain usable and the Client shall say that AI is unavailable.
