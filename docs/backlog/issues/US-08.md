## User story
As a student, my attempt does not change when a new lab version is published, and I am told a newer one exists.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-CAT-005`: An attempt shall be pinned at start to the bundle version, rubric version and evaluator version, and shall retain them for its lifetime.
- [ ] `FR-CAT-006`: Publishing a new version shall not change an in-progress attempt; the Client shall notify the student that a newer version exists and let the student start a new attempt.
