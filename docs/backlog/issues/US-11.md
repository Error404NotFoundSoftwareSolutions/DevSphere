## User story
As a student, I see which tools are ready, missing or incompatible, and nothing is reinstalled needlessly.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-ENV-001`: The Client shall detect the operating system, version, CPU architecture, free disk space and total and available memory.
- [ ] `FR-ENV-002`: The Client shall detect whether each prerequisite declared by the lab (at least Python, `uv` and Git) is installed and report its version and location.
- [ ] `FR-ENV-003`: The Client shall compare detected versions with the lab's declared constraints and report each prerequisite as ready, missing or incompatible.
- [ ] `FR-ENV-009`: A prerequisite that already satisfies the lab's constraints shall count as ready and shall not be reinstalled.
- [ ] `FR-ENV-012`: The student shall be able to re-run prerequisite checks after changing the environment.
- [ ] `FR-ENV-013`: The Client shall report an unsupported OS, architecture or tool version clearly and shall not present an untested configuration as supported.
