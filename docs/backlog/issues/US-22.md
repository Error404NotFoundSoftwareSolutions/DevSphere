## User story
As a student, my progress syncs without duplicates, I can work offline, and I can continue on another device.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-PROG-004`: The Client shall sync progress through the authenticated API using client-generated event IDs, with at-least-once delivery, and the backend shall apply each event idempotently.
- [ ] `FR-PROG-005`: Step completions shall merge as a set union across devices; verified results are server-authoritative and are never overwritten by Client data.
- [ ] `FR-PROG-006`: The Client shall show the last sync time and number of pending events, shall allow offline work on cached labs, and shall explain that verified grading and tutor use need connectivity.
- [ ] `FR-PROG-007`: After sign-in on another device, progress state and results shall be available; source files shall not be synchronised, and the Client shall say so.
- [ ] `NFR-REL-003`: Synchronisation shall recover from network loss without duplicate progress, submissions or points.
- [ ] `NFR-PERF-006`: Catalogue and progress lists shall use pagination or incremental loading.
- [ ] `NFR-PERF-007`: Output captured by the Client and Grader shall be size-limited to bound memory and disk use.
