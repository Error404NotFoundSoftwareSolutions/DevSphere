## User story
As a student, my setup readiness is shown apart from my score, and using the tutor never lowers my score.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-GRADE-013`: Environment readiness shall be reported separately from the lab score.
- [ ] `FR-GRADE-014`: The use of the tutor shall not reduce a score, and prompt count, token count and time spent shall not be score inputs.
