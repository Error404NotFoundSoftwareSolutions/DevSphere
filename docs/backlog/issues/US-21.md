## User story
As a student, my progress is saved after every step and survives a crash.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-PROG-001`: The system shall record, per attempt, the selected lab, state, completed steps and results.
- [ ] `FR-PROG-002`: The Client shall show labs as not started, in progress, completed or failed.
- [ ] `FR-PROG-003`: The Client shall save local progress after each completed step and each task or evaluation event, and shall recover it after an abnormal exit.
- [ ] `NFR-REL-001`: A failure in the workspace, a lab process or the tutor shall not crash the Client or lose saved progress.
- [ ] `NFR-REL-002`: The Client shall report a setup, submission or external action as successful only after confirming the resulting state.
