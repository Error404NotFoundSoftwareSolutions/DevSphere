## User story
As a student, I can download a lab, trust that it is unmodified, and open it offline.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-CAT-004`: Published lab bundles shall be immutable and checksummed, and the Client shall verify the checksum before use.
- [ ] `FR-CAT-007`: The backend shall deliver only published bundles that the student is authorised to access, and no bundle delivered to the Client shall contain protected tests or reference solutions.
- [ ] `FR-CAT-008`: The Client shall cache downloaded bundles for offline use and show each cached bundle's version and whether a newer version is available.
