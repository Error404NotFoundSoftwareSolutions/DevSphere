**Sprint:** 6  |  **Release:** MVP  |  **Priority:** P1 High  |  **Work order:** #30 of 60

## User story
As a student, I choose what the tutor sees and I give consent before my code is sent to a cloud provider.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-AI-001`: The Client shall provide a text tutor within the active lab.
- [ ] `FR-AI-004`: The tutor shall receive only the context the student has permitted for the session (current step, selected code, recent task output), shall default to minimal context, and the Client shall show what is shared.
- [ ] `FR-AI-012`: The Client shall show the active provider and model.
- [ ] `FR-AI-014`: The Client shall disclose that prompts and selected code are sent to a cloud AI provider and shall obtain consent before first use.
- [ ] `NFR-PRIV-001`: The Client shall disclose when code, prompts or logs leave the device and to which service.
- [ ] `NFR-PRIV-002`: The student shall be able to turn off sharing of workspace context with the tutor; the tutor shall continue to work from the question and current step.
