## User story
As a student, I see what data is collected and agree before I continue, so that I stay in control of my data.

## Requirements covered
- [x] `FR-PROF-001`: Before collecting any profile data, the Client shall present a privacy notice and record the student's consent with notice version and timestamp.
- [x] `FR-PROF-010`: The platform shall apply the age policy approved under TBD-08 at sign-up and shall record only the outcome of that check, not a date of birth.
