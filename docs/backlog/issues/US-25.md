## User story
As the team, AI requests pass through our backend that holds the key and enforces per-student limits.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-AI-002`: Cloud AI requests shall pass through a backend proxy that holds the provider credential; no AI provider key shall exist in the Client.
- [ ] `FR-AI-003`: The tutor shall use a provider interface so that a provider can be replaced without changing tutor policy or guide logic.
- [ ] `FR-AI-013`: The student shall be able to cancel a request, and the backend shall enforce per-user rate, request-size and time limits.
- [ ] `FR-AI-015`: The backend shall not retain prompt or response content beyond request handling unless the student opts in; retained metadata shall be limited to provider, model, status and latency.
- [ ] `NFR-COST-002`: AI and verified-grading usage shall be limited per student (defaults in TBD-13).
- [ ] `NFR-SEC-015`: The backend shall apply rate limits to sign-in, AI, submission and profile endpoints.
