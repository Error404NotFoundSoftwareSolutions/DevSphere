## User story
As a student, I can ask for my data to be exported or deleted, and the action is audited.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-PROF-009`: The platform shall provide a documented route for a student to request data export and account deletion; fulfilment procedures and timelines shall follow the retention policy (TBD-09).
- [ ] `NFR-SEC-013`: Security-relevant actions (role changes, publications, score corrections, data export/deletion) shall create audit events with actor, target, time and reason, and no secret values.
