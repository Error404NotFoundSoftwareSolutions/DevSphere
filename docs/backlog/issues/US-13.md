## User story
As a student, my project environment is created from the lab's lockfile, checked afterwards, and failures explain how to recover.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-ENV-007`: Project dependencies shall be installed into an isolated project environment using `uv` by default, with `venv` and `pip` as fallback, and shall honour the lab's lockfile.
- [ ] `FR-ENV-008`: After any setup step the Client shall verify success by re-detecting the tool and, where applicable, running a harmless validation command, and shall not report success without that verification.
- [ ] `FR-ENV-010`: When a setup step fails, the Client shall show the failure, recovery guidance and a log with secrets removed, and shall clean up or clearly report any partial state.
