**Sprint:** 8  |  **Release:** MVP  |  **Priority:** P1 High  |  **Work order:** #39 of 60

## User story
As a student, resubmitting never doubles my points, grader failures are not held against me, and I see queue status and history.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-GRADE-012`: Submitting the same snapshot hash for the same attempt, and retrying a grading job, shall not create a duplicate result or duplicate points.
- [ ] `FR-GRADE-015`: The system shall distinguish a student failure from an infrastructure error (Grader unavailable, timeout of the service); infrastructure errors shall not count against the student and shall allow retry.
- [ ] `FR-GRADE-016`: The backend shall limit verified submissions per student and attempt, show queued/running/completed status, and keep the history of submissions.
- [ ] `FR-GRADE-017`: The system shall retain enough non-secret evidence to explain why points were awarded or withheld.
- [ ] `NFR-REL-004`: Grading jobs shall be retried within a bounded policy after transient infrastructure failure, and failed jobs shall be visible to operators.
- [ ] `NFR-PERF-005`: For a standard MVP lab, a verified submission shall return a result within 120 seconds at the 95th percentile under pilot load (target; TBD-10).
- [ ] `NFR-TEST-002`: Verified results shall be reproducible for the same snapshot, bundle, rubric, evaluator and sandbox image, except for tests declared nondeterministic (none are permitted in the MVP).
