## User story
As the team, retention, legal review and access rules are approved before real students join.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `NFR-PRIV-004`: The platform shall document what data is stored, why, for how long and how to delete it, and shall approve a retention schedule before the pilot (TBD-09).
- [ ] `NFR-PRIV-005`: The platform shall complete a legal review covering applicable privacy law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) and the treatment of users under 18 before the pilot (TBD-08).
- [ ] `NFR-PRIV-006`: Student profile fields shall be limited to those in FR-PROF-003 unless a new field is approved through change control.
- [ ] `NFR-PRIV-007`: Stored student snapshots and records shall be accessible only to the owning student and authorised operators.
