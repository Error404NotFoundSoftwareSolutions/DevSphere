## User story
As a student, nothing risky or AI-proposed runs without my approval, and I am told which commands are tracked.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-EXEC-004`: The Client shall not claim knowledge of commands run in terminals it does not manage.
- [ ] `FR-EXEC-007`: The Client shall obtain consent before an action that deletes data, modifies files outside the lab workspace, or uses the network beyond what the lab declares.
- [ ] `FR-EXEC-008`: A command proposed by the tutor shall never run without explicit student approval.
