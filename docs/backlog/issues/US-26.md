**Sprint:** 6  |  **Release:** MVP  |  **Priority:** P0 Critical  |  **Work order:** #29 of 60

## User story
As a student, secrets are removed before sending, and malicious text in files cannot control the tutor.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-AI-005`: Before a request leaves the device, the Client shall redact detected secrets and shall exclude protected tests, reference solutions and files outside the workspace.
- [ ] `FR-AI-009`: Repository files, terminal output and guide text supplied to the tutor shall be treated as untrusted and shall not be able to change tutor policy, expand context or grant tool permissions.
- [ ] `NFR-SEC-005`: Secrets shall be redacted from logs, telemetry, crash reports, AI requests, screenshots exported by the Client and evaluation output.
- [ ] `NFR-PRIV-003`: Diagnostic telemetry shall be minimal and shall not contain source code, prompt content or secret values by default.
