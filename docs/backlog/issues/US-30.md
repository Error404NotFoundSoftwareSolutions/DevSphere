**Story ID:** US-30
**Sprint:** 7  |  **Release:** MVP  |  **Priority:** P0 Critical  |  **Work order:** #36 of 60

## User story
As a student, I get a Verified result computed from the grader's signed output, with per-check feedback that does not reveal tests.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-GRADE-001`: Each lab shall declare an evaluator and a versioned rubric.
- [ ] `FR-GRADE-008`: Each check result shall be one of passed, failed, skipped, blocked or not-applicable, with points, a reason code and student-safe feedback that does not reveal protected test source.
- [ ] `FR-GRADE-009`: The backend shall compute the score from the Grader's signed result; a score, rank, rubric version or evaluator version supplied by the Client shall be ignored.
- [ ] `FR-GRADE-010`: Each verified result shall record bundle version, rubric version, evaluator version, snapshot hash, sandbox image version and timestamp.
- [ ] `FR-GRADE-011`: Score records shall be immutable; a correction shall be a new record with actor, reason and timestamp.
