## User story
As an operator, I approve a lab, publish an immutable version, roll back by republishing, and keep a record of who published what.

## Requirements covered
- [ ] `FR-CONT-006`: On approval, CI shall publish an immutable, checksummed bundle to private bundle storage (delivered only through the authenticated API, FR-CAT-007) and protected tests only to private grader storage.
- [ ] `FR-CONT-007`: Rolling back a lab shall publish a new version containing the earlier content; historical versions shall be retained and not modified.
- [ ] `FR-CONT-008`: Each publication shall record the source commit, the approving person, the timestamp and the bundle checksum.
- [ ] `FR-CONT-009`: Lab content that references third-party service documentation shall carry a last-verified date, and CI shall flag entries older than the threshold in TBD-12.
- [ ] `NFR-MAINT-003`: All third-party dependencies shall be inventoried with version, licence and update owner.
- [ ] `NFR-LEGAL-001`: The product shall preserve licence notices and comply with the licences of integrated tools, libraries, fonts and content.
